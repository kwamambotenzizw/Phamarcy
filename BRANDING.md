# Thachi branding and login

The application retains the user's Thachi emblem and wordmark. The current selected asset is **web/brand-logo-current.png**, copied unchanged from the user's supplied `pasted-image-11.png`. It is an RGBA PNG with genuine transparent exterior pixels and is used by login, navigation, settings, invoices, delivery notes and the browser icon. Earlier supplied/generated files remain in `web/brand-logo.png`, `web/brand-logo-v2.jpeg` and `web/brand-logo-royal.png` for reference; they are no longer the active logo.

The previous royal-blue capsule variant was edited with the **built-in image_gen tool**, from the supplied WhatsApp logo. Its edit prompt was:

> Edit this exact Thachi Investments logo. Make ONLY the small tilted medicine capsule above the emblem royal blue #4169E1 instead of its current dark blue. Apply #4169E1 to the capsule's blue outline and filled top-right half, preserving its existing white bottom-left half and white highlight. Keep the large flowing T emblem, vertical divider and the exact words 'Thachi' and 'Investments' in their ORIGINAL existing blue colours, with unchanged typography, spacing, scale, position and geometry. Preserve the white background and 1280:640 horizontal composition. Do not redraw or redesign anything. No extra text, no effects, no shadows. The ONLY change is the existing small pill's blue areas changing to solid royal blue #4169E1.

Dark surfaces display the wordmark in white using CSS filtering and blending, with the coloured capsule overlaid in its original position. White surfaces and printed invoices retain the blue artwork. The application colour palette remains unchanged.

The login follows the supplied split-screen reference. The capsule decoration is CSS, separate from the actual company logo. Desktop presents the navy illustration panel and white form; mobile retains the company logo above the form. Password visibility toggles work; Remember me stores the email address only and does not extend authorization or store the password. Forgot password and Contact your administrator explain the existing administrator-managed recovery flow; no email reset service is connected. Demo credentials are available in the collapsed Demonstration access section, with an explicit Use demo account button.

The invoice template now supports the supplied business address/TIN, customer billing addresses, default Due Upon Receipt terms, decimal-comma amounts and a three-decimal tax percentage. New sales retain the approved tax percentage in their posted payload; older documents derive a display percentage from their recorded amounts. Changing today's tax setting never recalculates recorded invoice amounts. Reference invoice number 670 and its gloves line are demonstrated in `artifacts/invoice-template-sample.pdf`; that preview is not a posted financial transaction.
