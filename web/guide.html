Your Thachi Investiments guide

Sales, stock and finances share a single transaction ledger. Use the demonstration environment to review the workflows before configuring a live business.

Sales & payments

Sales → New sale. Search or scan a barcode, add units, choose a customer and invoice currency, then add payments in their receiving accounts’ currencies. Register a customer for credit. Prescription products require an authorized pharmacist and reference. Completed sales print as A4 invoices or thermal receipts.

Open an unpaid invoice to receive a partial/full payment. For corrections, an admin/manager issues a full credit note. Returns enter quarantine and require recorded inspection before resale.

Inventory & expenses

Receive stock using whole base units, supplier, batch, expiry and USD cost. For five packs of ten, enter 50 units. Stock controls allow recall, quarantine, counts and write-offs with a reason. Pay supplier invoices in Cash Flow.

Record operating expenses separately from inventory and assets. An unpaid expense creates a payable; payment creates the account outflow. Assets are capitalized. Depreciation is a preview until disposal, not a scheduled monthly posting.

Offline & reconciliation

Authorize the designated terminal online first. Only that terminal should sell during an outage. The encrypted outbox survives restart and is unlocked with the authorized password. Offline sales have provisional printable receipts. Financial summaries show the last synchronized snapshot.

Open the bell/sync link for pending, failed or conflicting events. Reconnect to retry. A manager must review conflicts, reconcile stock/funds, retain evidence and create any correction separately. Never clear pending local records.

Reports & backup

Dashboard → Export report opens P&L, cash flow, trial balance, audit, valuation, expiry and balances. Export CSV or print/save PDF. Settings → Download database backup requires an online administrator.

For detailed setup, backup/restore, user management, test evidence and incomplete features, read README.md, USER_GUIDE.md and TEST_RESULTS.md in the project folder. No payment gateway, external rate feed or production hosting is connected.

Reference application. Verify local tax and pharmaceutical rules, complete production deployment/security controls and review documented gaps before using live business or patient information.
